How EduMap works

Three steps, in order — each one only works because the step before it did.

1

Locate

Every registered school is placed on an interactive map, so gaps in coverage are visible at a glance.

2

Diagnose

Each school carries real indicators — ratios, water access, class sizes — that show exactly where help is needed.

3

Connect

Donors, NGOs, and government partners browse, filter, and reach out to the schools that match what they can offer.
